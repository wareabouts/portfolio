The assignment was to delete the audio from a clip from a video game and create new sounds for it. I love "music videos without music" and jumped at the opportunity to make my own. I downloaded the first minute of the video for Lematire's "Closer" and created or edited sounds in Logic Pro X, timing them along with the video. My clip is above on the left, Lematiere's original on the right.
