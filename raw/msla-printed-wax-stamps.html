MSLA wax stamps

I'm blown away by the resolution on my Elegoo Mars MSLA Printer. Smooth edges turn out smooth and the detail is incredible. Having visited a local stationary store, I was curious if that detail could be transferred into a wax stamp seal. A couple of tests later, it works but I've still got more to do.
Depths on the icons probably shouldn't exceed a millimeter and the logo extrusion should narrow some so the wax separates from the stamp more easily. The resin also doesn't have the thermal conductivity that metal does, meaning the stamp had to be left for a few seconds until it cooled. Attempts to take the stamp off earlier ripped off pieces of the wax.
