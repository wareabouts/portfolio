## What is it?

I used Blender to make a variety of animated intros for different Sauce+ shows, all done in Blender on absurd timelines. The Scare The Coyote one was made in just a few days, and made to appear like it was a tabletop diorama. Two headed chef was made digitally with cut up pieces of public domain art, and the Roadtrip Show was made in 24hrs and made to look like a claymation scene that pulled inspiration from various events / scenes in the show. 

## What was the goal, and who was it for?
Goal was to make high-quality animations... but by myself on tight timelines. 

## What did you actually do?

For all of them, modeled, textured and animated parts that came together for the final scene. I picked the music and cut it to fit each show.
